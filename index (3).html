Personal prototype, not a production product

Live ChatDashboard

The problem: agents handling WhatsApp conversations at volume need to know who is waiting, what was said and what they can do next, without hunting for it.

The thinking: WhatsApp's 24-hour messaging window decides what an agent can send, so it stays visible. Dispositions, internal notes and search keep the workflow operational, and customer numbers are masked for privacy.
